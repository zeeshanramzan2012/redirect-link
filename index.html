Know the signs

The 6 red flags of a phishing email

Scammers rely on you not looking closely. Slow down and check for these.

⏰

Urgency & pressure

"Act within 24 hours or your account will be suspended." Real requests rarely demand instant action.

✉️

Mismatched sender address

The display name says "SSGC HR" but the actual address is a strange, unrelated domain.

🔑

Requests for credentials

No legitimate system will ever email you asking to "confirm your password" via a link.

🎁

Too good to be true

Surprise bonuses, prizes, or refunds you didn't apply for are a classic bait.

📎

Unexpected attachments

An invoice, resume, or "signed document" you weren't expecting — especially .zip or .exe files.

🔤

Odd spelling & formatting

Awkward grammar, mismatched fonts, or a logo that looks slightly off from the real one.

House rules

The do's and don'ts

Simple habits that stop most phishing attempts cold.

✓ Do

  • Hover over links to preview the real URL before clicking
  • Type your organization's website address directly into the browser
  • Verify unusual requests by phone or in person with the sender
  • Report suspicious emails, even if you're not fully sure
  • Keep your device's software and antivirus up to date

✕ Don't

  • Click links or open attachments from unknown senders
  • Enter your username or password after clicking an email link
  • Reuse your work password on other websites
  • Forward suspicious emails to coworkers "just to check"
  • Assume a familiar logo means the email is genuine
Already clicked?

What to do next

If you clicked a suspicious link or entered your details, act fast — in this order.

Don't enter anything else

If a page asks for your password, don't type it — close the tab immediately.

Disconnect if you already entered credentials

Disconnect your device from the network and contact IT Security right away.

Report the email

Forward it to the security team using the address below — don't delete it first.

Change your password

If you entered your credentials anywhere, change your password immediately.
